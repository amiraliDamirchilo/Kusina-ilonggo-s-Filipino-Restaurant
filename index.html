Our Culinary Roots

Bringing the Flavorful Heritage of Iloilo to Ras Al Khaimah

Iloilo City in the Philippines is world-famous for its rich, slow-cooked soups, grilled specialties, and joyful food culture. At Kusina Ilonggo's Filipino Restaurant, we bring these exact authentic recipes straight to our community in Julan - Al Nakheel, Ras Al Khaimah.

Whether you are a Filipino kababayan looking for a taste of home or a local food enthusiast eager to discover authentic Southeast Asian comfort food, our doors are open with warm hospitality and freshly cooked dishes every single day.
